# Playmakxrs Existing Website Structural Model

This document outlines the structural layout, controls, content patterns, and visual design of the existing Playmakxrs website, extracted from the captured screenshots. This serves as a baseline model to evaluate and compare against future rebuilds.

## 1. Global Design & Aesthetics

- **Theme:** High contrast. Alternates between dark mode (black backgrounds) for hero/CTA sections and light mode (white or light gray backgrounds) for content sections.
- **Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Roboto). High readability, bold headers.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Backgrounds: True Black (`#000000`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Light Gray (`#F9F9F9` or similar).
  - Accent Color: Vibrant Red (approx. `#E53935` or `#FF0000`). Used extensively for highlighting keywords in headings, primary buttons, bullet points, and active states.
- **Controls & Interactive Elements:**
  - **Primary Buttons:** Solid red background with white text (e.g., "Contact Us", "Explore the Model").
  - **Secondary Buttons:** Transparent background with white or red borders/text (e.g., "Start a Conversation").
  - **Cards/Boxes:** Simple borders with slight padding, used to group information in grids.
  - **Lists:** Custom bullet points using red horizontal dashes (`—`).

## 2. Global Components

### Header / Navbar (Sticky)
- **Left:** Brand Logo ("PLAYMAKXRS GLOBAL™").
- **Center/Right (Navigation):** Inline links: `About`, `For Institutions`, `Pilot`, `Partnership`, `The Book`, `Collection`.
- **Right (CTA):** Solid red "Contact Us" button.

### Footer
- **Left Column:** Logo, taglines, brief brand statement, and an email newsletter subscription form (Text input + Red "Subscribe" button).
- **Middle Column ("Quick Links"):** Links to all main pages.
- **Right Column ("Connect"):** Contact email and social media icons (Facebook, TikTok, Instagram).
- **Bottom:** Copyright text and a "Back to Top" floating red circular button with an upward chevron.

## 3. Page-by-Page Content & Structure

### Home (`/`)
- **Hero:** Dark background. Bold heading with red highlight. Two buttons: Red primary ("Explore the Model"), bordered secondary ("Start a Conversation").
- **Sections:**
  - Text-heavy explanatory sections alternating between white and light gray backgrounds.
  - Custom red-dash bulleted lists to highlight key value propositions ("Why This Matters Now").
  - A horizontal row of outlined pills/tags ("Protect", "Verify", "Govern", "Activate", "Compound").
  - **Media feature ("The Founding Proof Case"):** Highlights a specific story with CTA buttons.
  - **Social Proof:** "As Featured In" section displaying logos of major networks and brands (ESPN, FOX, Amazon, etc.).

### About Us (`/about-us`)
- **Hero:** Dark background, descriptive tagline.
- **Content:** Deep dive into the company's philosophy and origin story.
- **Component - Asset Grid:** A 3x2 grid of cards ("One Story, Many Assets"). Each card has a red category label (ARTICLE, VIDEO, BOOK), title, and source. 
- **Component - "What We Are Not":** A 3x2 grid of dark-themed, outlined boxes clearly defining boundaries (e.g., "A PR campaign", "A viral content shop").

### For Institutions (`/for-institutions`)
- **Hero:** Dark background, institutional focus. Primary CTA button.
- **Component - Target Audience Grid:** 4x2 grid of simple outlined white boxes listing roles (e.g., University Presidents, Trustees).
- **Component - Process Cards:** 5 vertical cards showing a step-by-step engagement process (Numbered 1-5 with red numerals).
- **Component - FAQ Accordion:** List of frequently asked questions. Each row has a red `+` icon on the right side indicating expandability.

### Pilot (`/pilot`)
- **Hero:** Dark background. Focus on the "90-120 Day INI™ Pilot".
- **Sections:**
  - Heavy use of the red-dash bulleted lists for "What We Assess" and "What Success Looks Like".
  - **Component - Role Grid:** 3x2 grid of outlined boxes detailing who is involved.
  - **Component - Deliverables Grid:** 4x2 grid (interrupted by logo) showing exact deliverables (e.g., "Narrative Capital™ Map").

### Partnership (`/partnership`)
- **Hero:** Dark background.
- **Component - Comparison Columns:** A clear two-column layout comparing "Partnership Is" (red dashes) vs. "Partnership Is Not" (gray dashes) to establish expectations.

### The Book (`/the-book`)
- **Hero:** Dark background. Features a large image of the book cover ("No Grier, No Game") on the left, with title and "Order the Book" CTA on the right.
- **Component - Value Grid:** 3x2 grid of outlined boxes highlighting enduring themes of the story.
- **Component - Retailer Buttons:** Side-by-side buttons for purchasing (Solid red "Amazon", Bordered red "Barnes & Noble").

### Collection (`/collection`)
- **Hero:** Dark background. Focus on "Participation, Not Promotion".
- **Component - Guiding Principles Grid:** 4 horizontal outlined boxes laying out the philosophy of the merchandise.

### Contact Us (`/contact-us`)
- **Hero:** Dark background. Title: "Start a Conversation".
- **Component - Complex Form:** 
  - Standard inputs (First Name, Last Name, Organization, Email, Website).
  - Dropdown Selectors ("I am reaching out as *", "I am interested in *").
  - Required Message Textarea.
  - Section for "Optional Questions" with four additional large textareas for deeper inquiry context.
  - Full-width red "SUBMIT INQUIRY" button at the bottom.
